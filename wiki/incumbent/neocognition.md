## What it is

Stealth AI research lab spun out from Ohio State University NLP/agents group. **$40M seed round April 2026** (Cambium + Walden Capital). ~15 PhDs. Led by **Yu Su** (OSU, NLP and language agents — knowledge graphs, web agents, retrieval). Public framing: *"self-learning agents that build a world model of work."* Pitch references *"agents that can self-learn... similar to how humans learn"* — metaphor without mechanism specification.

**Architecture is undisclosed.** Stealth phase. Public materials are recruiting-shaped and funding-announcement-shaped.
