## Drift to flag

**(1) Heavy stale-source noise after bulk-ingest days.** 172 stale-source findings dominate this run. They're not bugs — the rule is correctly flagging "citing page touched, cited source not re-verified." But the signal-to-noise ratio is poor on days with multiple ingests touching dozens of pages. Two possible refinements worth discussing:
   - Severity tier the rule: warn only if `last_ingested` delta > N days
   - Filter the report by default: surface stale-source counts but only list when delta > 30 days, with `--all` flag for full output
   
   Either change would make the post-bulk-ingest lint actionable. Defer to AJ; per `feedback_discipline_sustainability` the empirical test is "does the count feel grinding."

**(2) Missing-construct-validity on biology source pages.** 55 findings, ~30 of which are biology pages (Josselyn, Nader, Tse, Yang, Schacter, etc.). The rule was designed for AI/ML metrics where construct validity is a real epistemic concern ("does this metric measure what we claim?"). For experimental neuroscience, the construct is typically the experimental paradigm itself — `[MEASURED]` on "rats in hippocampal-lesion group failed to learn over 13 sessions" doesn't need a separate construct-validity note; the paradigm IS the construct.

Two options:
   - Source-page exemption: rule 8 applies only to concept/decision/hypothesis/experiment pages, not source. Source pages can quote `[MEASURED]` claims from a paper without authoring fresh construct-validity statements (the paper presumably has its own discussion). 
   - Tag-side change: introduce a new tag (e.g. `[MEASURED-PAPER]`) for measurements quoted from a source, distinct from `[MEASURED]` for Kyrja-internal measurements. The new tag wouldn't trigger rule 8.
   
   Option 1 is the cheaper fix and probably the right call. Defer to AJ.

**(3) Three concept/incumbent pages with empty `sources:`.** Real bugs introduced by parallel ingest sessions (lora, retrieval-granularity, ami-labs) — pages were created without populating frontmatter. Not blocking but should be cleaned up before the next lint pass.
