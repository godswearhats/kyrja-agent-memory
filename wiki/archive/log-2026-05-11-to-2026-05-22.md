### 2026-05-17 — Ingest — Hu et al. 2021 LoRA paper + caddy commitment-4 nomenclature clarification

Nils (indigo). LoRA deep-dive session driven by AJ wanting to understand the LoRA architecture given how often it's referenced as load-bearing-or-inspirational for caddy design. Full verbatim read of Hu et al. 2021 (arXiv:2106.09685, ICLR 2022). The session surfaced one consequential and two non-consequential findings:

**Consequential finding (§ 7.3 amplification):** ΔW operates on the *subordinate* singular directions of W (Frobenius norm 0.32 on ΔW-chosen directions vs 21.67 on W's own top-r), amplifying them ~21.5×. **LoRA cannot create features W doesn't already encode** — only amplify under-emphasised latent capabilities. This is the load-bearing finding for caddy: it sharpens the capability distinction between cross-attention (D2/D3) and adapter/LoRA modulation (D4) interfaces — they are *capability-different*, not just stylistically different.

**Non-consequential findings (§ 7.1 spread-thin, § 7.2 intrinsic-1-dim):** real paper findings, anchored on the LoRA concept page, but assessed during the session as low-relevance to caddy MVP. § 7.1 is about matrix-type spread within a layer, not layer-depth spread (the actually-interesting axis for caddy). § 7.2's intrinsic-1-dim → "salience signal C should be scalar" was rejected as a cross-domain leap with no actual evidence value, and would have violated the deferred-decision discipline that holds C's signature open.

**The handwave correction.** Three places in the caddy spec treated "cross-attention or adapter/LoRA modulation" as interchangeable interface options: caddy commitment 4, caddy-architecture op I1, caddy-interface-doors D-table intro. AJ proposed the lens "we used that pair as shorthand for connects-at-the-weights-level tech, not as a claim of equivalence" — and the lens survived doc review: the D-doors framework already does the structural split (D2/D3 = cross-attention vs D4 = adapter/LoRA modulation are enumerated distinctly). The fix was nomenclature, not structural.

## 2026-05-17 — Reservoir computing literature-review pass (Nils, side-quest window)

Nils (indigo). Side-quest window companion to the main-window M14 walk earlier today. AJ asked for an 8-paper deep read of the reservoir-computing literature to anchor or close the open-question [reservoir-computing](../open-question/reservoir-computing.md) that the main-window walk had promoted from speculation. 5 papers read verbatim (Lukoševičius & Jaeger 2009 review, Maass et al. 2002 LSM, Pathak et al. 2018 KS-prediction, Pascanu & Jaeger 2011 working memory, Sussillo & Abbott 2009 FORCE); 2 read at abstract level (S4, Yamazaki-Tanaka 2007); Mamba already had a source page from prior work.

**Five load-bearing findings the ingest preserves:**

1. **Pure RC ("fixed substrate + linear readout") is empirically dead for the tasks a caddy would need.** Both Pascanu/Jaeger 2011 (working memory) and Sussillo/Abbott 2009 (autonomous pattern generation) — papers from inside the RC community — extended the textbook architecture to handle their tasks. Pascanu/Jaeger added WM-units with trainable feedback into the reservoir; Sussillo/Abbott added FORCE online training with feedback control, and their 1C variant trains inner reservoir synapses. The architectural commitment that motivated the M14-implementation analogy is the commitment the RC field itself voted against. Sketch A in the open-question doc is `[REJECTED]`.

2. **The biological RC precedent (cerebellum) is FEEDFORWARD, not chaotic-recurrent.** Yamazaki & Tanaka 2007 abstract states the granular layer "generates a finite but very long sequence of active neuron populations *without recurrence*." This is structurally different from the canonical ESN/LSM model. The hippocampus is densely recurrent (CA3 in particular); the cerebellar template does not transfer directly. The "RC is the natural extension of Buzsáki's framing" claim is weaker than the open-question doc originally framed. The cerebellum-as-feedforward-LSM is structurally closer to LoRA-on-frozen-base than to a chaotic reservoir.

3. **The SSM lineage (S4 → Mamba) is the engineering-side abandonment of RC's commitments.** S4 trains the A matrix (with HiPPO structured initialisation); Mamba makes A input-dependent (selectivity). The arc is: random fixed → structured fixed → structured trained → structured trained input-conditioned. Each step abandoned more pure-RC purity and got better results. RC's lineage is alive in form (linear state-space framework), dead in commitment (fixed random substrate). The new genealogy table is in [source/gu-dao-2023-mamba § RC genealogy](../source/gu-dao-2023-mamba.md#rc-genealogy-added-2026-05-17).

4. **Quantitative anchors that update Kyrja's "what RC actually does" model:** Pathak's 8 Lyapunov times on Kuramoto-Sivashinsky PDE (with parallel reservoirs of 5000 neurons each, scale-invariant via spatial decomposition) is *prediction-horizon*, not memory-capacity. Memory capacity is provably linear in N (C ≈ Nλ/(1−λ) per Jaeger's measure) — sets a hard upper bound on any pure-RC substrate. The spectral-radius < 1 condition for the echo-state property is explicitly called out as folklore by both Lukoševičius/Jaeger 2009 *and* Pascanu/Jaeger 2011 (citing Buehner & Young 2006 for refined algebraic conditions). Any wiki claim using "ρ<1" as load-bearing should be re-anchored. Pathak used ρ=0.6 for KS; Pascanu/Jaeger used ρ=0.5 for WM — task-dependent in practice, not the textbook 0.9.

5. **Pure linear readouts are empirically insufficient even for clean prediction tasks.** Pathak 2018 required a quadratic P₂·r² term — pure linear failed because of a parity-symmetry conflict with the KS equation. Combined with Pascanu/Jaeger's WM-units extension and FORCE's non-linear feedback control, this softens the [catastrophic-interference](../concept/catastrophic-interference.md) page's "RC bindings are purely linear" framing. Re-anchored with caveats in this ingest.

**Caddy-decision implication:** Sketch A (pure RC as caddy substrate) is empirically dead. Sketch B (reservoir-as-pre-training) is partially instantiated by S4. **Sketch C (reservoir + writable buffer + off-line consolidation) remains the architecturally most-interesting candidate** because it is the one the literature does not address. It maps cleanly onto the M14 SPW-R off-line operational regime and is structurally distinct from both the SSM lineage and current memory-system designs.

## 2026-05-16 (main window) — Matrix walk M12 + M13: quasi-regular handling, temporal context, primitives-not-independent observation (Nils)

### M12 — quasi-regular handling

Biology walked: McClelland's English past-tense argument (regular *walk → walked* rule plus exception *go → went*) as the canonical example requiring both episodic specifics and structural regularity simultaneously. U-shaped developmental learning curve as the evidence. The deeper claim: any real-world cognitive domain is quasi-regular; one system cannot do both jobs because the learning rates are structurally incompatible.

DB analog landed cleanly: **OLAP + OLTP problem with ETL pipeline.** Engineering converged on the same architectural separation as biology — episodic transaction store for specifics, accumulated analytical representation for regularities, periodic consolidation moving stable patterns between them.

**AI status:** bolt-on ❌ (episodes only, no abstracted regularity representation); substrate ❌ (structure only, pretrained regularities baked in, no episodic specifics). Only row in the walk so far where both family members have hard ❌ on *opposite halves* — other ❌ rows are missing one capability; M12 is missing perpendicular capabilities in each family member.

**Caddy reading:** could have both natively — episodic store (commitment 1) carries specifics; learned representation (commitment 5's auxiliary objective shapes the regularity layer) carries patterns; consolidation policy (commitment 2) moves stable regularities into the representation while preserving exceptions. Practical use case: long-running personal-assistant scenarios where the regularity plane is the agent's accumulated user/domain understanding persisting across sessions, and the episodic plane is memory of specific events. Both queryable on every interaction.

### M13 — temporal context as retrieval primitive

Biology walked: Howard & Kahana 2002 TCM. Every memory trace encoded with the current temporal context bound in — context is a vector drifting slowly, carrying the "smell" of recent events. Retrieval reinstates bound context, which then biases the next retrieval toward items whose stored contexts overlap. Temporal contiguity effect (and its forward asymmetry) falls out of the mechanism for free.

Cross-link to write-side cousin: M13 plus the time cells (MacDonald 2011) and conjunctive coding ([[cognitive-maps-and-conjunctive-coding]]) documented from M11 give the full *what + where + when* hippocampal binding signature. M13 is the read-side primitive that lets context-as-cue retrieve the bound trace.

Algorithmic crossover: TCM is mathematically close to the **successor representation** in RL (Stachenfeld et al. 2017). Both formalise "encode state along with a predictive-context smear." Useful pointer if temporal-context primitives become load-bearing — there is a parallel RL literature with relevant tooling.

DB analog: closest to nothing in mainstream engineering. Distributed-tracing parent pointers (discrete, not continuous) and shingled indexing (records encoded with recent-history fingerprints) are the closest fragments; no system combines them into a continuous drifting-context primitive.

**AI status:** substrate ❌ as a *persistent* mechanism (attention provides within-window temporal position; evaporates cross-session); bolt-on ❌ as a vector primitive (timestamps as metadata, recency as scalar weight). Caddy: could have natively — maintain a drifting context vector as part of caddy state, bind it into every episodic trace at write time, allow retrieval to cue on context similarity. H41 already captures the falsifier specification.

### M10 — Reconsolidation (retrieval-triggered update window)

Biology walked: Nader, Schafe & LeDoux 2000 protein-synthesis-inhibitor experiment establishing that retrieval briefly destabilises previously consolidated memories. ~1-6h labile window during which the memory is sensitive to modification; reconsolidation re-stabilises it (or fails — see PTSD/trauma failure modes). Read-as-write semantics: every retrieval is an opportunity to update.

AJ's framing landed quickly: opt-in hooked retrieval (engineering's obvious solution) is uncontroversial. The interesting question is what biological-faithful reconsolidation buys beyond the controlled version. Candidate advantages surfaced:

- **Schema migration** — old memories getting re-encoded into new schemas as they're retrieved. Opt-in hooked requires writing a "this memory now fits a new schema" detector; reconsolidation gets it for free.
- **Contextual strengthening** — biological testing effect; use-in-context strengthens use-in-this-context. Engineering versions (LRU/LFU) strengthen "this got used" but not "this got used for this kind of query."
- **Compression-via-use** — reconsolidation as continuous compression pressure under scarcity (cross-link M09 scarcity-is-generative). Every retrieval is a compression opportunity.
- **Unifying principle:** reconsolidation makes *use* a learning signal. Structurally the same shift as RLHF → self-supervised learning: from sparse explicit supervision to dense implicit supervision. For agents with sparse explicit feedback, may be load-bearing.

AJ's tag: "Eldar-level tech" — can't bolt-on later. First architectural property the matrix walk surfaced where the family-level framing may be wobbling. Coupling-depth axis flagged as a candidate refinement: read-as-write requires *intrinsic* coupling of read and write operations, which third-path can have natively and bolt-on can chain but not couple.

### M11 — Catastrophic interference avoidance via interleaved learning

Initial framing was ML-flavoured (McClelland's argument). AJ called this out: "you started a section with the title 'biology' but it was mostly ML." Redo from the actual biology produced the substantive content of this walkthrough.

**The biology proper:**
- Sharp-wave ripples in hippocampus during slow-wave sleep — ~150-250 Hz, 50-100ms bursts
- Wilson & McNaughton 1994: maze-running sequences from waking re-appear in subsequent-sleep hippocampal recordings — foundational direct evidence of replay
- Temporal compression: ~10s waking trajectory replays in ~100ms during a ripple (~100×)
- Reverse replay (Foster & Wilson 2006): after salient events (most prominently reward), replay runs *from outcome backwards* through the preceding trajectory
- Hippocampal-cortical dialogue: ripples timed to cortical up-states when cortex is depolarised and receptive
- McClelland 1995's *deeper* argument: dual-system architecture is forced by joint pressure of generalisation (distributed reps) vs interference-resistance (sparse reps); you can't have both in one system

**Place cells aren't really place cells.** This was new ground:
- **Conjunctive coding** (Wood, Dudchenko & Eichenbaum 1999): atomic encoding unit is place × event × context, not single-dimension. Most cells require specific combinations of cues to fire.
- **Time cells** (MacDonald 2011): same hippocampal architecture encodes elapsed time when place is held constant.
- **Remapping**: same cells fire at different "places" in different contexts; alternate populations active in different contexts.
- **Cognitive maps generalise** (Constantinescu, O'Reilly & Behrens 2016 Science; Aronov, Nevers & Tank 2017 Nature): grid-cell-style coding tiles abstract feature spaces, sound-frequency spaces, conceptual 2D spaces. Hippocampal mechanism is map-of-relations; physical space is the evolutionarily ancient instance.
- **Multiple overlapping maps**: one event has coordinates in many parallel maps simultaneously.

**Reward/salience firing:** Goal cells (Hollup 2001), replay over-represents rewarded trajectories (Singer & Frank 2009), dopaminergic modulation of plasticity (VTA/Schultz 1997). Salience is a coordinate axis in the binding structure, not metadata.

### AJ-originated reframings that landed during M11

Four substantive reframings produced during the dig, each capture-worthy:

1. **Hot store as training-data source for the cold store.** Tighter than McClelland 1995's own framing. Cold storage's structure is determined entirely by what gets replayed, in what order, at what rate. The hot store is the *generator* of the training distribution, not a temporary buffer.

2. **Non-i.i.d., non-deterministic, open-world, trajectory-shaped training data.** Biological replay violates every standard ML training assumption. Cortex is trajectory-trained, not dataset-trained; non-reproducible (two identical twins with identical lives would have different cortical states); training/inference distinction collapses. The frozen-snapshot ML deployment model is the alien thing, not biology.

3. **Reverse replay = automatic root cause analysis / distributed tracing on memory.** Engineering re-framing of Foster & Wilson 2006's credit-assignment interpretation. A third-path memory module implementing reverse-replay-on-salience would emit causal traces as a structural property of the architecture; outcomes get automatically linked to precursors during consolidation, no hand-coded annotation pass.

4. **Schema induction, not schema implementation.** The biology isn't implementing a schema; it is inducing one. The hippocampus didn't come pre-loaded with "store place + event + outcome." Those structured codings emerged from neural plasticity. A bolt-on system commits the schema upfront; a third-path system can let it emerge.

### The cross-examination — bolt-on can also induce schemas

Initial claim from Nils: "bolt-on commits to a storage representation upfront and so structurally cannot do schema induction." AJ pressure-tested it: "let's say bolt-on was the only option, could I create a memory system that sits between agent and LLM which could induce its own schema?"

The claim didn't survive. Four buildable mechanisms by which bolt-on *can* induce schemas:

1. Learned embedding space — fine-tune embeddings on retrieval-success episodes
2. Learned field structure / extraction — meta-model over the agent-LLM stream discovers conjunctions
3. Learned salience / consolidation policy — PER-shape at write time
4. Learned retrieval policy — learned routing instead of fixed kNN

The honest refinement: **the real distinguisher is integration-depth, not capability-binary.** AJ named it: "the primitives for training would be different." Bolt-on decomposes the problem into four sub-systems with separate training loops; third-path can train one model end-to-end with gradient flowing through. Different bets on where engineering complexity lives.

Updated list of genuinely third-path-only properties (after cross-examine, held loosely):
- End-to-end gradient flow through memory (storage must be differentiable)
- Read-as-write coupling at zero latency (M10 reconsolidation primitive)
- Continuous online schema drift (vs scheduled retraining boundaries)
