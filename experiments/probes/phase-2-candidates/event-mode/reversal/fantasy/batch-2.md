# Reversal × Fantasy — Batch 2 (Tier B)

**R-Fa2-1**

The pyre stands ready in the courtyard, stacked waist-high with juniper and birch, the condemned woman bound at its centre. The Tribunal of Flame has pronounced sentence. Three inquisitors voted guilty; the required majority is two. The executioner holds the torch. The crowd presses against the railings. The High Cleric raises her hand to signal the lighting — and then lowers it. A runner has come through the east gate, still mounted, carrying a sealed cylinder. The High Cleric breaks the seal and reads aloud: the Archon of the Northern Reach has invoked the Writ of Sanctuary, a prerogative used twice in living memory, claiming the condemned woman as a sanctioned oracle under the Archon's protection. The Writ supersedes the Tribunal's jurisdiction. The executioner steps back. The bindings are cut. The condemned woman stands in the unlit pyre, breathing.

**R-Fa2-2**

"The walls will not hold," Sergeant Byrne says, and I believe him. I have been on the parapet since dawn, and I can count the fires in the valley — the siege host numbers four thousand at least. We are ninety-one, and the well ran dry yesterday. The gate timbers are cracked from the ram. Captain Aldric has written the surrender terms himself, in his own hand, on the only clean parchment left in the keep. I watched him seal it.

He never sends it. A horn sounds from the northern ridge — not their horn. Ours. The banner that crests the hill belongs to the Margrave of Dunfeld, whose relief column we petitioned six weeks ago and who sent back a single word: "No." But his cavalry is pouring down the slope now, three hundred riders in column, hitting the siege camp from the rear. I can see the surrender letter still sitting on the captain's desk through the open window. The seal is unbroken.

**R-Fa2-3**

The guild elders sit in the crescent chamber, and Idris kneels before them on the examination stone. He has failed the Proving twice. No apprentice in the history of the Weavers' Guild has been granted a third attempt — the bylaws are explicit, and the First Weaver read them aloud when Idris entered the chamber. His master refused to speak on his behalf this morning. The other apprentices cleared his workbench before the hearing began, stacking his tools in the crate reserved for those expelled. The First Weaver opens the guild ledger to record the dismissal. She dips her pen. Then the Second Weaver stands. He places a cloth on the table — a tapestry fragment Idris wove during his second Proving, the one that was ruled insufficient. The Second Weaver has identified in it a lost binding pattern from the guild's founding texts, a technique no living weaver can replicate. The First Weaver sets her pen down. The elders examine the fragment, passing it hand to hand. The First Weaver closes the ledger without writing in it. She tells Idris to rise. His tools are returned to his workbench by evening.

**R-Fa2-4**

The dragon's body cools in the pass. Prince Callum stands over it, sword still drawn, his bannermen arranging the trophy — the severed horn, the scorched shield that blocked the killing blow, the position of his boots when the blade struck home. The bards are already composing. The prince sends a rider ahead to the capital with the news: the beast is slain, the eastern villages are safe, and the prince will enter the city at midday. He expects the gates open and the bells ringing.

The rider returns within the hour. The king's herald is with him. The herald dismounts and reads from a scroll: the prince is charged with treason for leading an armed company beyond the kingdom's borders without the sovereign's seal. The dragon's territory was claimed by the Tyvari Compact, and the prince's incursion constitutes an act of war. The prince is to surrender his sword and return to the capital under guard. The bannermen lower the trophy. Callum hands over his blade.

> A = Prince Callum; G = return to the capital as a hero for slaying the dragon; X = triumph (dragon slain, trophy prepared, bards composing, rider sent ahead to announce victory); ¬X = charged with treason, ordered to surrender his sword and return under guard. ✓ polar binary on triumph-vs-disgrace axis, ✓ expectation set before resolution, ✓ inversion acknowledged within event.

**R-Fa2-5**

*By order of the High Conclave of Selith, recorded this seventh day of the Waning Moon:*

*The enchantress Maren of Greyholt, having been summoned before this body to answer the charge of practising forbidden summoning arts, did present herself willingly. Six witnesses testified to her use of proscribed binding circles. The Conclave's own wardkeeper confirmed residual void-traces in her tower consistent with summoning-class incantations. The accused offered no defence and spoke only to request a swift judgement. The Arbiter of the Conclave consulted the precedent scrolls and prepared the writ of banishment.*

*Before the writ could be read, the Archseer rose and addressed the chamber. She presented a sealed report from the Conclave's boundary wardens: a rift had opened in the northern marches three days prior, and the void-traces in Maren's tower matched the signature of a counter-seal — not a summoning, but a closure. The binding circles were containment wards. The Archseer stated that without Maren's intervention, the rift would have breached the outer boundary within hours.*

*The Conclave voted unanimously to vacate all charges. The writ of banishment was burned in the chamber brazier. Maren of Greyholt departed with the Conclave's formal commendation.*
