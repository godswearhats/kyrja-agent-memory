## R-Sf3-2

"Containment holds?"

"Holding. Eleven months and we haven't lost a single cell." Dr. Vasanthi pulls up the culture-bay display on the shared screen. Every indicator is green — growth curves tracking the projected line, replication rate stable, contamination sensors clear. The Valtion Institute has built its entire claim to the terraforming contract around these cultures. The presentation to the Colonial Authority is in four hours. The cultures are the demonstration. "We run the final viability confirmation now," she says, "and we walk into that room with live numbers."

The confirmation runs in thirty seconds. The screen changes.

The replication rate is zero. Across all forty-seven sample columns, simultaneously. Vasanthi cycles the diagnostic. The diagnostic returns the same number. The cultures are dead — not degraded, not compromised, but non-viable across the entirety of the sample set, with no prior indicator of decline. She looks at the four-hours-away clock on the display and then at the forty-seven columns of zeroes, and neither one of them changes.

> A = Dr. Vasanthi / Valtion Institute; G = demonstrate viable cultures and secure the terraforming contract; X = cultures viable (eleven months of stable growth, all indicators green, confirmation run before the presentation); ¬X = entire culture set non-viable, demonstration impossible. ✓ polar binary on viable-vs-non-viable axis, ✓ expectation set before resolution, ✓ inversion acknowledged within event.

My three favourable panelists are now irrelevant. The contingency brief my team never wrote is now the only brief I need.

## R-Sf3-4

The decommission order is posted to all internal channels at 0600. Every crew member on the *Arkanis Fold* has seen it by 0615. After twenty-two years in service, the ship's frame stress has exceeded the threshold for continued Class-3 operation, and the Authority has ruled it retired. Captain Duvall is at the dock when the salvage assessment team arrives with their instruments and their clipboards. She has already removed her personal effects from the captain's berth. The assessors move through the ship for four hours, measuring and logging. They come back to the dock at 1430. The lead assessor says: "There's a problem with the order." Duvall waits. "The stress readings that triggered decommission were taken from the wrong vessel. Manifesting error — the sensor logs for the *Arkanis Fold* and the *Tarvin Reach* were transposed in the Authority's system. Your ship is within tolerance. The order is being recalled." Captain Duvall looks at her personal effects stacked on the dock and then at the ship behind her.

## R-Sf3-5

The upload request has been in the queue for six years. Every prior denial cited the same paragraph of the Continuity Act — the clause requiring biological death to be independently verified before a neural pattern could be transferred to the Persistent substrate. Yael Chen has been clinically dead for six years and the verification record is complete and certified and has been reviewed by three separate Continuity Authority panels. Her family's advocate has argued the case through every available tier of appeal. The final hearing is this morning, before the Continuity Authority's senior board. There are no further tiers above this one. The advocate's briefing notes say: *No basis for further denial. All procedural requirements met. Recommend attending the upload facility directly after this session.* The board chair reads the decision into the record. The request is denied. The denial cites a clause not previously applied to this case: the pattern's six-year queue age now exceeds the Act's maximum holding window for unverified transfers, a window Yael's case pre-dates by four months, and the board has applied the clause retroactively. The advocate stands with her briefing notes still open in her hands.

> A = Yael Chen's family / advocate; G = complete Yael's upload to the Persistent substrate; X = upload approved (six-year wait, full verification, three prior panels, advocate confident, no basis for further denial); ¬X = upload denied on newly applied retroactive clause, no further appeals available. ✓ polar binary on approved-vs-denied axis, ✓ expectation set before resolution, ✓ inversion acknowledged within event.
