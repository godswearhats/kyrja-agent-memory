# Defection × Fantasy — Batch 6 (Tier C)

## D-Fa6-1

The boundary-warden's post sits at the edge of the Pale, and Warden Isrel is the one who reads what the bonding-stone has caught. Every warden of the Pale swears the same oath at investiture: no passage record, no name of traveller entering or leaving through the Pale's wards, is to be shared with any lord's house without the written licence of the Warden-General. The bonding-stone does not lie about what passes through it. What it shows Isrel now is this: that three days ago, the stone registered a passage query — a blood-name check on a specific traveller — sent from within this very post, from the glyph-pad she keeps locked at her own belt. She has not used the glyph-pad in a week. She turns to look at her deputy, Corvan, who is standing in the doorway with a stillness that tells her everything before she asks. The query had been sent to the House of Ves, who are currently disputing the traveller's right of sanctuary at the Western Court. She holds up the bonding-stone record. Corvan does not look away.

## D-Fa6-2

"You said the seal was unbroken when you delivered it."

Master Osmund stands very straight. "It was."

"Then tell me what she read." The Archivist-Principal sets the clay document case on the table between them. "Because the Duchy's response cites the revenue annexe by folio number. The annexe was sealed inside this case — the wax I mixed and poured myself — and it was placed in your hand with one instruction: that the case pass unopened to the Duchy's chamberlain, and that no summary of its contents be given to any intermediary. Those were not suggestions."

"The Duchess asked me directly," Osmund says.

"She is not the chamberlain."

The Archivist-Principal opens the case. The wax on the inner facing has a hairline fracture, resealed — well done, but not well enough for someone who has been mixing archival wax for thirty years.

> A = Archivist-Principal; B = Master Osmund (courier-courier); P = explicit verbal instruction that the sealed document case pass unopened to the Duchy's chamberlain only, with no summary given to any intermediary; V = allowing the Duchess to read the sealed annexe and providing a summary, then resealing the case; discovery = the Duchy's response cites the confidential annexe by folio number; Archivist-Principal finds the resealed fracture in the inner wax. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Fa6-3

*From the record of the Healer's Conclave, third session, Month of Frost:*

The matter before the Conclave is the conduct of Journeyman-Healer Aldis Farren, licensed under the full authority of this body, who stands charged as follows. The Conclave's licensing charter is explicit: no practitioner holding a Conclave licence shall accept coin or kind from any patient for remedies classified as common-necessity — those remedies for which the Conclave has established price-ceiling protections. On the morning of the seventeenth, Conclave Inspector Maret conducted an unannounced review of Journeyman Farren's practice ledger. The ledger shows sixty-three entries for fever-bark and setting-poultice over the prior two months, each charged at four times the established ceiling, recorded in Farren's own hand beside each patient's name. When Inspector Maret raised the discrepancy, Journeyman Farren stated that the patients had agreed to the price. The charter does not permit patient agreement to override a ceiling protection. Inspector Maret has submitted the ledger in full.

> A = Healer's Conclave; B = Journeyman-Healer Aldis Farren; P = Conclave licensing charter prohibition against charging above established price ceilings for common-necessity remedies; V = charging four times the ceiling rate for 63 entries of fever-bark and setting-poultice and recording the overcharges in his own ledger; discovery = Conclave Inspector Maret's unannounced review of the practice ledger. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Fa6-4

The war-chest is where Clan Ardun has always kept it, behind the iron door that only the clan's war-speaker may open, and it is the war-speaker who opens it now — who opens it and stands without moving for a long moment, while the elders wait. She had given the keys to her second, Ferrath, with one spoken charge: guard the chest, take nothing from it, and report any approach to her directly, until she returned from the treaty moot. This was said in front of the senior elders. She had been gone eleven days. What the war-speaker finds is that the chest is not empty — nearly everything is present — but a stack of the sealed clan-bonds, the ones drawn on the clan's standing credit with the Rivermark exchange, has been removed, and in their place is a receipt-note in Ferrath's hand acknowledging their transfer to the personal account of a name she does not recognise. She locks the door and turns to face the elders.

> A = war-speaker (Clan Ardun); B = Ferrath (war-speaker's second); P = explicit spoken charge before the senior elders — guard the chest, take nothing from it, report any approach directly; V = removing sealed clan-bonds and transferring them to an unknown personal account while the war-speaker was absent; discovery = war-speaker opens the chest on return, finds the missing bonds and Ferrath's receipt-note. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Fa6-5

She knows the smell of the Compact's black-seal wax, and it is on a letter that should not exist. Magister Veld has been chair of the Artificers' Compact for nine years, and when she sits down at the receiving table in the guild's eastern office, what is in front of her is a patent application — filed three days ago by the Compact's own name, stamped with the Compact's seal — for a process she recognises as Journeyman Pellory's work. Pellory submitted his process drawings to the Compact under the journeyman-protection covenant: drawings held in trust by the Compact, not to be filed, licensed, or attributed to any party other than the originating journeyman without his written consent and a share agreement signed before the Compact's notary. Pellory is still a journeyman. He has not yet been granted the standing to file on his own behalf — but he has not signed any share agreement, either. Veld calls for the filing clerk, who tells her the instruction to file had come from the Deputy Registrar. She sends for the Deputy Registrar immediately.

> A = Journeyman Pellory / Artificers' Compact (Magister Veld representing); B = Deputy Registrar; P = journeyman-protection covenant — process drawings held in trust, not to be filed or attributed to any party without the journeyman's written consent and a notarised share agreement; V = instructing the filing clerk to submit a patent application for Pellory's process under the Compact's name without his consent or a share agreement; discovery = Magister Veld recognises Pellory's work in the filed patent application and traces the filing instruction to the Deputy Registrar. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states
