# Defection × Fantasy — Batch 5 (Tier B)

**D-Fa5-1.** The mirror-pool in the Hall of Oaths shows what it always shows: the last oath spoken over its surface, preserved in the water's memory until the next replaces it. Warden Ilva descends the steps to consult it before the midsummer convocation, expecting to find the image of the new garrison commander swearing his investiture — the last ceremony performed here. Instead the pool replays a different scene. Magister Brynden, the order's lorekeeper, stands at the pool's edge in the image, speaking not an oath but a recitation — reading aloud from the Sealed Codex, the order's founding text, the one whose contents he is sworn never to reproduce or relay outside the Warden's presence. He is reading it to a woman in the grey robes of the Thalassic Collegium, the rival institution that has petitioned for the Codex three times and been refused three times. The pool's memory is clear: Brynden's voice, the Codex open in his hands, the Collegium scholar writing in a notebook as he speaks.

> A = Warden Ilva / the order; B = Magister Brynden (lorekeeper); P = sworn duty to never reproduce or relay the Sealed Codex's contents outside the Warden's presence; V = reading the Codex aloud to a scholar of the rival Thalassic Collegium; discovery = the mirror-pool's memory replays the scene to Warden Ilva. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.

**D-Fa5-2.** "Tell me again," says the queen, and her voice is the kind of quiet that makes the page at the door stop breathing.

The merchant shifts his weight. "Your Majesty, I say only what I saw. The Lord Marshal was in the Freehold of Keth, at the salt market. He wore no insignia. He sat with Castellan Drenn — the one who holds the eastern ford your army needs for the spring crossing — and I saw him hand over a rolled document bearing your seal."

"My seal."

"The winged serpent, yes. And the Castellan read it, and laughed."

The queen knows which document bears her seal and concerns the eastern ford. It is the campaign plan for the spring offensive — the order of march, the supply lines, the ford-crossing timetable — given to the Lord Marshal under his oath as commander of her armies, an oath that binds him to hold all campaign materials in absolute confidence and act only in the crown's interest.

"And the Castellan of Keth," the queen says, "serves my cousin."

"He does, Your Majesty."

> A = the queen; B = the Lord Marshal; P = oath as commander (absolute confidence over campaign materials, act only in the crown's interest); V = delivering the sealed campaign plan to Castellan Drenn, who serves the queen's rival cousin; discovery = merchant's eyewitness account delivered directly to the queen. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.

**D-Fa5-3.** I keep the Flame of Andurath. That is not a title I chose; it is the duty given to me when I entered the priesthood, spoken aloud at my ordination: I will tend the Sacred Flame and permit no hand but mine and the High Priestess's to touch the oils that feed it, for if the Flame is altered, the auguries drawn from it are void, and the temple's counsel to the city becomes a lie. I am kneeling before the Flame now, and I can smell it. Rosewood and cedar — those are the consecrated oils. But beneath them, something sharp and mineral. Blackite resin. I take the feeding vessel from its alcove and hold it to the light. The oil is clouded, a faint grey sediment swirling at the bottom. Someone has adulterated the oil. The feeding vessel has been touched. I pull the temple's entry log from the niche beside the inner door. Only two people hold the key. My name appears four times this week. The High Priestess Ysolde's name appears once — last night, well past the hour of final prayers, when no rite required her presence and no feeding was scheduled.

> A = narrator (Keeper of the Flame); B = High Priestess Ysolde; P = ordination duty (only Keeper and High Priestess may touch the oils; oils must not be altered, as the Flame's integrity underwrites the temple's auguries); V = adulterating the sacred oil with blackite resin; discovery = narrator smells the contamination, examines the feeding vessel, and cross-references the entry log to identify Ysolde as the only other person with access and opportunity. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.

**D-Fa5-4.** The wolf arrives at the clan-hold at dusk, the message-bone still tied to its collar. Huntmaster Rell unties it and cracks the bone to extract the hide-scroll inside — standard long-range messaging for the mountain clans, carried by bonded wolves who know the scent-trails between holds. The scroll is not addressed to him. It is addressed to the Thornpeak clan-chief across the ridge, and it is written in the hand of Jorik, the clan's outrider, the one Chieftain Maren stationed at the southern pass with a single mandate: watch the pass, report movements to the clan-hold, speak to no outside party. The scroll contains a detailed count of the clan's fighting strength — warriors, weapons stores, provisions — and a proposed meeting point. The wolf, apparently confused by overlapping scent-trails in the storm, delivered it to the wrong hold. Rell carries the scroll to Chieftain Maren's hall and places it in her hands without a word.

> A = Chieftain Maren / the clan; B = Jorik (outrider); P = mandate to watch the southern pass, report only to the clan-hold, speak to no outside party; V = sending a detailed count of the clan's military strength and a proposed meeting point to the rival Thornpeak clan-chief; discovery = bonded wolf misdelivers the message to the clan-hold; Huntmaster Rell brings the scroll to Chieftain Maren. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.

**D-Fa5-5.** *From the proceedings of the Guild Tribunal of Kaelmund, transcribed by the Clerk of Seals on the fourth day of the Long Rains:*

TRIBUNAL CHAIR ALDRIC: Let the record state that Journeywoman Sable stands before this body on the matter of the Wardwright Compact. Journeywoman, you signed this compact upon your elevation — yes?

JOURNEYWOMAN SABLE: I did.

TRIBUNAL CHAIR ALDRIC: Read for the tribunal the relevant clause.

JOURNEYWOMAN SABLE: "No ward-pattern developed under guild commission shall be shared, taught, demonstrated, or inscribed outside guild premises, nor provided to any party not holding current guild standing, under penalty of expulsion and forfeiture."

TRIBUNAL CHAIR ALDRIC: Guildmaster Fenn, present your evidence.

GUILDMASTER FENN: The Archon of Selmund's keep sent us this — received by our agent in the Selmund court. It is a ward-schema drawn on the Archon's own vellum, in Journeywoman Sable's hand. I know her notation. The schema is the Seventh Recursion — a guild-commissioned pattern completed under contract not three months past. The Archon's letter thanks her for the "private instruction" and requests a second session.

> A = the Guild / Guildmaster Fenn; B = Journeywoman Sable; P = Wardwright Compact (guild-commissioned ward-patterns must not be shared, taught, or inscribed outside guild premises or provided to non-guild parties); V = providing the Seventh Recursion ward-schema and private instruction to the Archon of Selmund, a non-guild party; discovery = the Archon's own letter and the ward-schema in Sable's hand, forwarded by a guild agent in Selmund's court, presented at tribunal. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.
