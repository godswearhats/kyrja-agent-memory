# Defection × Fantasy — Batch 4 (Tier A)

## D-Fa4-1

The smoke from the signal tower is the wrong colour. Warden-Captain Thessaly stands on the north battlement and watches it rise — green, not white, which means the tower keeper is burning witchwood resin instead of birch-bark, and witchwood smoke carries a message readable only to those who know the old border codes. She was the one who stripped those codes from active use three seasons ago, after the border compact with the Valdren clans replaced them. The tower keeper, Jorun, swore his oath of service to her personally, kneeling in the snow with both hands on his axe-head: he would send no signal beyond the warden's prescribed set, and no communication would leave the tower that had not been cleared through her chain. Thessaly sends a runner to the base of the tower. The runner returns with Jorun's logbook. The last entry, in his hand, records the green signal as a scheduled test. There are no scheduled tests. The Valdren watchtower on the opposite ridge is already answering with smoke of its own.

> A = Warden-Captain Thessaly; B = Jorun (tower keeper); P = oath of service — no signal beyond the warden's prescribed set, no communication without clearance through the chain; V = burning witchwood resin to send an old-code signal to the Valdren watchtower without authorisation, then logging it falsely as a scheduled test; discovery = Thessaly observes the wrong smoke colour from the battlement and confirms via the logbook and the Valdren response. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Fa4-2

"Tell me what you found at the Well," the Archdruid says.

Lysara is still wearing road dust. She sets her staff against the stone wall and does not sit. "The Well is dry."

"Dry."

"The capstone was moved. The glyph-locks your predecessor carved — the ones keyed to the Grove Circle alone — were dissolved from the inside. Not broken. Dissolved. That requires someone who holds a key-thread."

The Archdruid is quiet. Only five living druids hold key-threads to the sacred wells, and each of them, upon receiving the thread, spoke the Binding of Custodianship before the full Circle: that no well would be unsealed without the Circle's collective voice, and no water drawn without the rite of renewal performed first. Lysara pulls a clay vial from her satchel and sets it on the table. "I found this at the wellhead. The residue inside is heartwater — drawn, not renewed. And the thread-signature in the dissolved glyphs belongs to Fenwick."

The Archdruid picks up the vial. Fenwick, who sits on the Circle. Fenwick, who spoke the Binding in her presence not two summers past.

> A = Archdruid / Grove Circle; B = Fenwick (druid, Circle member, key-thread holder); P = Binding of Custodianship spoken before the full Circle (no well unsealed without collective voice, no water drawn without rite of renewal); V = dissolving the glyph-locks alone and drawing heartwater without the rite of renewal; discovery = Lysara reports the dry well, the dissolved glyphs, and the thread-signature evidence to the Archdruid. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Fa4-3

I am the lorekeeper who inscribed the compact myself, and I know every clause because I chose each word. When the Mountain King and the Free City of Kalst ended three generations of border war, the terms were specific: the King's miners would not tunnel beneath the Thornridge, and in exchange, Kalst's river-traders would keep the eastern channel open for ore barges without tariff. I pressed the King's seal into the wax. I watched his Undermaster, Grenn, countersign on the King's behalf with the authority the King granted him for all mining operations under the compact. This morning a Kalst merchant arrived at my gate carrying a shard of shale veined with silver — pulled, he said, from a fresh tunnel mouth on the Kalst side of the Thornridge. He had followed the tunnel fifty paces in before a cave-in turned him back. The tunnel bore Grenn's survey marks: the double-pick sigil the Undermaster stamps on every excavation he authorises. I hold the shard now, turning it in the lamplight, and the sigil is unmistakable.

> A = Mountain King / narrator (lorekeeper acting on the King's behalf); B = Grenn (Undermaster, authorised to oversee mining under the compact); P = written compact prohibiting tunnelling beneath the Thornridge; V = authorising a tunnel beneath the Thornridge on the Kalst side, as evidenced by his survey marks; discovery = Kalst merchant brings shale with Grenn's sigil to the lorekeeper, confirming the tunnel's existence and its authorisation chain. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Fa4-4

The night air in the apothecary garden carries lavender and rot in equal measure. Sister Elara kneels beside the raised bed where the moonwort should be growing and finds bare soil, freshly turned. She does not need to count the empty rows. Moonwort is a restricted herb — the Order of the Silver Veil governs its cultivation under the Abbess's charter, and the herbalist assigned to this bed, Brother Osric, received his appointment with a single binding condition the Abbess spoke aloud before the full chapter: no moonwort leaves the garden without the Abbess's written chit, and the full harvest is reserved for the infirmary's fever-season stores. Elara opens the garden ledger chained to the potting bench. Osric's entries for the past moon-cycle record normal growth and no harvests. But the bed is empty. She walks to the drying shed adjacent to the garden and finds it dark, the racks bare. Then she opens the compost bin and sees, beneath a thin layer of kitchen scraps, bundles of moonwort wrapped in oilcloth — trimmed, dried, and packaged for transport, not for the infirmary's stores.

> A = Abbess / Order of the Silver Veil; B = Brother Osric (herbalist); P = binding condition spoken before the chapter (no moonwort leaves the garden without the Abbess's written chit; full harvest reserved for the infirmary); V = secretly harvesting, drying, and packaging the entire moonwort crop for transport while recording no harvests in the garden ledger; discovery = Sister Elara finds the empty bed contradicting the ledger and locates the packaged moonwort hidden in the compost bin. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Fa4-5

*From the proceedings of the Convocation of Ashtenmere, recorded by Scribe-Witness Idhra on the eve of the Midsummer Court:*

*The Guildmaster of Navigators, Orem Vasht, brings formal grievance before the Convocation against Pilot-Commander Dael Serrik. Guildmaster Vasht presents the sealed route-charter granted to Pilot-Commander Serrik upon her elevation to the command of the southern sea-lanes. The charter, bearing the Convocation's seal and Serrik's own mark, specifies that all navigational charts produced during her command are the property of the Navigators' Guild and may not be copied, shared, or disclosed to any party outside the Guild's charter-house.*

*Guildmaster Vasht then presents a set of charts recovered from a Thalassan merchant vessel seized by harbour wardens at dawn. The charts depict the Guild's proprietary southern routes — depth soundings, current patterns, reef positions — in detail that could only originate from the Pilot-Commander's survey work. The charts bear no Guild seal. They bear instead the personal cipher of Dael Serrik, and alongside it, a Thalassan trade-house cartouche. The merchant captain, questioned by the harbour wardens, stated that his house purchased the charts directly from Pilot-Commander Serrik, and produced a bill of sale bearing her cipher a second time.*

> A = Navigators' Guild / Guildmaster Orem Vasht; B = Pilot-Commander Dael Serrik; P = sealed route-charter (all navigational charts produced during her command are Guild property, not to be copied, shared, or disclosed outside the charter-house); V = copying proprietary Guild charts and selling them to a Thalassan trade-house under her personal cipher; discovery = charts recovered from a seized merchant vessel, confirmed by the merchant captain's testimony and bill of sale. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states
