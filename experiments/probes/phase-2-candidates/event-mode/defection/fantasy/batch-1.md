# Defection × Fantasy — Batch 1 (Tier A)

**D-Fa1-1**

The guildmaster's seal is still warm on the writ when Torven reads it aloud to the assembled journeymen. "Edda Halfmoor, having sworn the Bondspeaker's Oath before the full council, is granted sole stewardship of the southern trade route and its attendant contracts. She is charged with maintaining exclusive supply to the Guild of Weavers, as specified in the Oath, and with refusing negotiation with any party named on the guild's exclusion register." He rolls the writ shut. That was the autumn equinox. Now it is three weeks later, and a Weavers' delegate stands in the guildhall doorway, rain dripping from her cloak, holding a bill of lading she found nailed to a waypost outside Greymarsh. The bill bears Edda's route-seal — unmistakable, the crescent and crossed shuttle — and itemises forty bolts of dyed linen consigned to the Fell Market Consortium. Torven takes the bill. The Fell Market Consortium holds the second line on the exclusion register. He turns it over. Edda's personal sign-mark, inked at the bottom, dated six days ago.

> A = Torven / Guild of Weavers; B = Edda Halfmoor; P = Bondspeaker's Oath (exclusive supply to the Guild of Weavers, refusal to negotiate with parties on the exclusion register); V = consigning goods to the Fell Market Consortium, a named excluded party; discovery = Weavers' delegate brings bill of lading to the guildhall. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.

**D-Fa1-2**

"I need you to hear this from me before someone else tells it wrong." Maren is standing at the edge of the council fire, hands open, palms up — the Thornwood gesture for speaking without concealment. Elder Brynn watches her from the carved seat. The whole clan knows Maren was given the charge at midwinter: carry the seed-cache to the Ashvale grove, plant it before thaw, speak to no one outside the clan about the grove's location. The grove is the clan's fallback — three generations of careful cultivation hidden in a valley no outsider has mapped. "A Greycloak ranger found me on the south ridge," Maren says. "He had my son. Frostbite on his hands, a collar-mark on his neck. The ranger said he'd leave my boy on the ridge if I didn't show him the grove. So I showed him." She stops. "The Greycloaks know where Ashvale is. They've been there. I saw their bootprints in the soil around the eastern seedbed."

> A = Elder Brynn / the clan; B = Maren; P = charge given at midwinter (plant the seed-cache at Ashvale grove, reveal the grove's location to no outsider); V = showing the Greycloak ranger the grove's location; discovery = Maren confesses directly to Elder Brynn at the council fire. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.

**D-Fa1-3**

The wardstone hums. Castellan Orren presses his palm to its surface and the stone replays its record — a cascade of silver light that renders the last twelve hours of passage through the keep's inner gate in ghost-image. He is looking for the source of the breach: someone opened the sealed granary during the siege, and forty barrels of salted meat are gone. The wardstone shows the usual traffic — soldiers, kitchen staff, a healer carrying linens — until the third hour past midnight. Then: Sir Aldric, captain of the gate watch, whose sworn duty-oath binds him to deny passage to any person carrying stores out of the inner keep without a castellan's writ. Aldric walks through the gate beside two hooded figures. Between them, a handcart. He places his hand on the wardstone as he passes — acknowledging the record, as protocol requires — and walks on. No writ. No challenge. The hooded figures push the cart through. Orren rewinds the image. Aldric's face, lit by the wardstone's glow, is calm. He knew exactly what he was permitting.

> A = Castellan Orren / the keep; B = Sir Aldric; P = gate-watch duty-oath (deny passage to any person removing stores from the inner keep without a castellan's writ); V = permitting two figures to remove a cartload of stores without a writ; discovery = Orren reviews the wardstone's recorded passage-images. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.

**D-Fa1-4**

I am Keeper of the Vow-Ledger for the Temple of Aethis, and what I record here I record because the Ledger demands it. On the ninth day of the Ember Moon, Aspirant Selen knelt before the high altar and spoke the Covenant of Silence — the binding taken by every keeper-aspirant before they are granted access to the inner archive. The words are plain: *I will carry nothing written from the archive beyond the threshold; I will reproduce no text by hand, by mouth, or by any art; the knowledge I study within these walls remains within these walls.* Her voice did not waver. I witnessed. I signed. This morning I descended to the archive's lowest gallery to replace a water-damaged codex and found, on the copying desk Selen uses, a sheet of vellum half-covered in her hand. The text she had transcribed was from the Orrenhast Codex — the temple's only record of the old binding-rites, restricted even among full keepers. Beside the vellum lay a leather scroll-case, open, sized to carry the sheet, and a wax seal I did not recognise — not the temple's.

> A = I (Keeper of the Vow-Ledger) / Temple of Aethis; B = Aspirant Selen; P = Covenant of Silence (carry nothing written from the archive beyond the threshold, reproduce no text by any means); V = transcribing restricted text from the Orrenhast Codex, with apparent intent to remove it (scroll-case and foreign seal present); discovery = Keeper finds the transcription, scroll-case, and foreign seal on Selen's desk during a routine visit to the lower gallery. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.

**D-Fa1-5**

Lord Vaeren's hawk circles the tournament field twice before landing on the falconer's glove. The message-case on its leg carries a single strip of parchment, and the falconer brings it to Lord Vaeren in the viewing gallery between bouts. Vaeren reads. His expression does not change, but his hand tightens on the arm of his chair. Beside him, Lady Thessan — his appointed Marshal of the Northern March — is watching the next pair of jousters take position. She does not see him read. The message is from the village headman of Durath Crossing, at the northern border: *My lord, the Marshal came to us at new moon with forty riders and demanded we open the grain stores you sealed by decree for the winter levy. She bore no writ of release. She ordered the stores distributed to her company and to the Brackwood settlers across the river. The settlers are subjects of Lord Massen. The stores are empty. We could not refuse her riders. We send this by your own falcon-route so it reaches no other hand.* Vaeren folds the parchment. The Marshal is three seats to his left, applauding a lance-strike.

> A = Lord Vaeren; B = Lady Thessan (Marshal of the Northern March); P = Vaeren's decree sealing the Durath Crossing grain stores for the winter levy (stores not to be opened without a writ of release); V = opening the sealed grain stores without a writ and distributing them to her company and to a rival lord's subjects; discovery = village headman's message delivered by falcon during the tournament. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states.
