# Defection × Sci-fi — Batch 3 (Tier C)

## D-Sf3-1

The integrity sweep runs automatically every six station-hours, and it is the sweep that catches it. Commander Vasquez is in her quarters when the alert reaches her wristband: anomalous outbound tight-beam, duration eleven seconds, from the relay node in Section 7. She pulls the transmission log on her display. The node is assigned to Chief Signal Officer Doran, whose signed post-assignment charter — the one every signals officer accepts at the start of a deep-station rotation — prohibits any outbound tight-beam contact with vessels not registered on the station's approved communication manifest without command-level authorisation. The receiving identifier in the log is a beacon prefix she has seen before: the independent survey consortium that Orbital Authority flagged as a restricted contact six weeks ago, when the mineral rights dispute went to arbitration. Eleven seconds is enough for a handshake and a data packet. She locks Doran's terminal and routes the log to the arbitration ombudsperson.

## D-Sf3-2

"Tell me what the environmental index read when you filed the Stage 3 certification."

The terraforming lead, Soo-Yeon, does not answer immediately.

"Because I have the raw sensor feed from the atmospheric array here." Director Okafor sets his datapad on the table between them. "And Stage 3 certification requires a sustained methane-conversion index above 0.74 for no fewer than thirty consecutive measurement cycles. Your filed certification says the index cleared 0.74 on cycle thirty." He turns the datapad to face her. "The raw feed shows it peaked at 0.71 on cycle twenty-eight and dropped to 0.68 on cycle twenty-nine. Cycle thirty was manually overridden. Your access token."

Soo-Yeon looks at the feed. The protocol requirement existed for a single reason: Stage 3 certification unlocked the settler intake order, authorising the first wave of permanent habitation. She had signed the certification herself, under the planetary development charter.

"The colonists board in nine days," Okafor says.

> A = Director Okafor / planetary development authority; B = Soo-Yeon (terraforming lead); P = planetary development charter requirement that Stage 3 certification is filed only upon genuine 30-cycle sustained atmospheric index above 0.74; V = manually overriding the sensor record on cycle thirty and filing false certification; discovery = Director Okafor cross-references the filed certification against the unedited raw sensor feed. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Sf3-3

I had attached the medic's uplink certificate to his personnel file with my own hands — which is the only way I know that what I am reading now is wrong. The certificate granted Brecken access to the ship's biometric archive under a single constraint: patient records were to remain within the ship's closed network, not transmitted externally without written consent from each patient and countersignature from me, the chief medical officer. We are three weeks out from Kepler Station, no external nodes reachable, so the alert that woke me at 0340 should have been impossible: uplink activity, Brecken's credentials, a packet addressed to a commercial genomics clearing-house. I pulled the packet header. Anonymised, he'd call it, if I ask him. One hundred and twelve crew members, seven months of biometric readings, their mitochondrial haplogroup flags included. The clearing-house has a purchasing relationship with two insurance underwriters I recognise from the ethics briefings. I go to wake Brecken now.

> A = chief medical officer (narrator); B = Brecken (ship's medic); P = uplink certificate constraining patient records to the ship's closed network (external transmission requires individual patient consent and CMO countersignature); V = transmitting 112 crew members' biometric and genomic data to a commercial clearing-house without consent or countersignature; discovery = 0340 uplink alert reviewed by narrator; packet header examined. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Sf3-4

The governance council's summons reaches Provisional Administrator Leng mid-session, and she reads it there at the dais while the water-rights petitioners wait. The summons cites a specific provision of the colony charter: the administrator's office holds no authority to enter into resource-extraction agreements with off-world entities without ratification by the full council. The council had not been convened. The agreement in question — a forty-year subsurface-mining licence covering the northern aquifer basin, signed with Extraction Syndicate Velan under Leng's seal — had been transmitted to the Syndicate's orbital relay four days prior. The council's data archivist had found it in the outbound treaty register during a routine index, and had flagged it to the council chair before dawn. Leng does not look up from the summons for a long time.

> A = governance council; B = Provisional Administrator Leng; P = colony charter provision requiring full council ratification before any off-world resource-extraction agreement is entered into; V = unilaterally signing and transmitting a 40-year mining licence to Extraction Syndicate Velan without council ratification; discovery = council's data archivist finds the agreement in the outbound treaty register and flags it to the council chair. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Sf3-5

The drone courier arrives at the mediator's platform with a sealed capsule from the Reach Collective's orbital station — standard diplomatic pouch, marked for the mediator's eyes only, in accordance with the ceasefire protocol both parties had accepted: all inter-faction communications during the arbitration period were to be routed exclusively through the mediator's platform, with neither faction making direct contact with the other. The Collective's Station Director, Priam, had given his spoken assurance to the mediator in the opening session, recorded in the arbitration transcript. Inside the capsule is a data chip containing a private message thread between Priam's station and the opposing faction's fleet commodore — forty-seven exchanges, the oldest predating the ceasefire by three days, the most recent timestamped six hours ago. The mediator sets the chip on the reader tray and opens the arbitration transcript to the session where Priam made his assurance.

> A = mediator; B = Priam (Reach Collective Station Director); P = spoken assurance given in arbitration session that all inter-faction communications would be routed exclusively through the mediator's platform during the ceasefire; V = conducting 47 direct private exchanges with the opposing faction's fleet commodore outside the mediator's platform; discovery = diplomatic pouch delivered to mediator contains the private message thread. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states
