# Defection × Sci-fi — Batch 4 (Tier A)

## D-Sf4-1

The habitat's atmospheric processor hums at a frequency Reva Osei knows by ear — she calibrated it herself before handing stewardship to Chief Environmentalist Pell under a resource-sharing compact signed by all six domes of the Enceladus settlement. The compact is specific: atmospheric output from Dome Three's processor is to be distributed equally across all six domes via the shared conduit network, and no dome administrator may reroute flow without a unanimous vote of the settlement council. Reva is reviewing conduit pressure readings from her engineering console in Dome One when she notices the differential. Domes Two through Six show a twelve-percent drop in airflow. Dome Three is running at full capacity — the processor has not failed. She traces the conduit routing and finds a manual override valve, activated from Dome Three's local controls under Pell's biometric signature, diverting the surplus into Dome Three's reserve tanks. The override was set forty hours ago. No council vote appears in the settlement log.

## D-Sf4-2

"Run it again," Yuki says.

The lab AI repeats the provenance trace, and the result does not change. Yuki is the genomics lead aboard the generation ship *Kairos*, and what she is looking at is the breeding-line register for Cohort Nine — the cohort currently gestating in the ship's ectogenic bays. Dr. Liam Vasik, the reproductive-ethics officer, was appointed by the ship's charter specifically to enforce the genetic-diversity protocol: no allele cluster may exceed fourteen percent representation in any cohort, and the randomisation algorithm must run unmodified before each fertilisation cycle. His role exists for this purpose alone. The provenance trace shows that the randomisation algorithm was run for Cohort Nine, but a secondary process — launched from Vasik's terminal, under his credentials — reweighted the output before the fertilisation commands were issued. The reweighting pushed three allele clusters above twenty percent. Yuki pulls Vasik's terminal log. The secondary process is timestamped six minutes after the randomisation completed.

> A = ship's charter / Yuki (genomics lead); B = Dr. Liam Vasik (reproductive-ethics officer); P = charter-mandated role-based duty to enforce the genetic-diversity protocol (no allele cluster above fourteen percent, randomisation algorithm unmodified); V = running a secondary reweighting process from his terminal that pushed three allele clusters above twenty percent before fertilisation; discovery = Yuki's provenance trace reveals the secondary reweighting process under Vasik's credentials. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

## D-Sf4-3

I am the one who gave him access to the relay. Kai and I were the only two signal officers on Outpost Varanasi, and when Central Command encrypted the new tightbeam protocols, I walked him through the key-exchange ceremony myself. He placed his hand on the console and spoke the commissioning oath aloud, the one that binds every signal officer in the network: no transmission leaves this relay without dual-officer authorisation, and no external frequency is to be opened without a logged directive from Central. Last night I woke to a vibration in the relay housing — a resonance I have felt only once before, during a burst transmission test. The console log shows a ninety-second tightbeam pulse, outbound, aimed at coordinates that do not correspond to any node in Central's network. Single-officer authorisation. Kai's biometric. No directive on file. The transmission content is encrypted with his half of the key pair alone, which means I cannot read it, which means Central cannot read it, which means it was composed to be invisible to every party in the chain except the recipient.

> A = narrator (signal officer) / Central Command; B = Kai (signal officer); P = commissioning oath (no transmission without dual-officer authorisation, no external frequency without a logged Central directive); V = sending a single-authorised tightbeam pulse to non-network coordinates with no Central directive, encrypted to exclude all chain parties; discovery = narrator wakes to the relay vibration and reads the console log showing Kai's solo-authorised outbound transmission. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states

*Sector Superintendent Mirela Denic holds delegated authority over water-reclamation distribution for Growing Sectors 5 through 9 under Cooperative Mandate 1140, which stipulates that recycled water output must follow the rationing schedule published by the Authority each quarter. Sector 7's grain yield projections for the current cycle are now unrecoverable.*

## D-Sf4-5

The quarantine buoy is still broadcasting its perimeter signal — a steady amber pulse that marks the exclusion zone around the derelict station — when Pilot-Inspector Maren Solberg pulls alongside in her patrol skiff and sees the docking clamp engaged. Someone is already inside. The exclusion order was issued by the Tri-System Health Authority after a prion-analogue contamination event, and the station was sealed under the Authority's emergency protocol. Dr. Costel Radescu, the epidemiologist assigned to manage the containment, accepted the sealing mandate in a recorded session before the Authority's oversight board: no entry by any party until the decontamination window expired, and he alone held the override codes for the docking seal. Maren pulls the clamp registry from the buoy's local cache. One entry. Radescu's override code. Timestamp: nineteen hours ago. She patches through to the Authority's watch desk and reads the entry aloud. Then she angles her skiff's floodlight through the station's observation port and sees, inside the contaminated lab module, sample canisters stacked on a cargo pallet that was not there when the station was sealed.

> A = Tri-System Health Authority / Pilot-Inspector Maren Solberg; B = Dr. Costel Radescu (epidemiologist, containment manager); P = sealing mandate accepted before the oversight board (no entry until the decontamination window expires; Radescu holds the override codes as custodian, not for personal use); V = using his override code to enter the sealed station and stage sample canisters for removal during an active quarantine; discovery = Solberg finds the engaged docking clamp, pulls the buoy's clamp registry showing Radescu's code, and observes the staged canisters through the observation port. ✓ all roles, ✓ all states
