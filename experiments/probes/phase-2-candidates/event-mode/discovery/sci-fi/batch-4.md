# Discovery × Sci-fi — Batch 4 (Tier A)

**Di-Sf4-1.** Navigation Officer Tova Lindgren pulls up the long-range cartographic overlay for the Cygnus transit corridor and lays it against the live sensor feed. She has plotted every convoy through this corridor for two years using the Admiralty's gravitational-hazard map — the one that shows a clean, low-mass passage between the binary pair at Cygnus-61. That map is the reason the corridor exists: it is the only route that avoids the tidal shear zones on either side, and every fuel budget, every transit schedule, every cargo insurance calculation for the sector is built on its mass profile. The live sensor feed is showing something different. The gravimetric distortion pattern ahead does not match a clean passage between two bodies. It matches a three-body system. There is a third mass source — dark, compact, directly in the mapped corridor — pulling at the convoy's trajectory in a direction the chart says should be empty. The clean passage she has been routing convoys through is a gravitational channel shaped by an object the Admiralty's map does not contain, and every transit she has plotted has been threading between three bodies, not two, with margins she never calculated because she did not know the third body was there.

> A = Navigation Officer Tova Lindgren; B = the Cygnus transit corridor is a clean, low-mass passage between a binary pair (per the Admiralty's gravitational-hazard map, basis of all route planning and fuel budgets); E = live sensor feed showing gravimetric distortion consistent with a three-body system — a third dark compact mass source directly in the corridor that the map does not show; B′ = the corridor contains a third gravitational body absent from the charts — every transit has been threading an unmapped three-body channel with uncalculated margins. ✓ all four roles, ✓ all three states.

**Di-Sf4-2.** "Yara, how many colonists are currently in cryostasis on Deck Fourteen?"

"Four hundred and twelve. Same as embarkation manifest. I run the biometric sweep every shift."

"And the power draw for Deck Fourteen — what's nominal?"

"Sixty-two kilowatts. It's been steady since we left Ganymede."

Chief Systems Engineer Daan Visser turns his console toward her and points at the power-distribution graph. "Deck Fourteen is drawing nine kilowatts. It's been drawing nine kilowatts since week three of the transit."

Yara stares at the graph. "That can't — the biometric sweep shows four hundred and twelve active signatures."

"The biometric sweep reads the monitoring chips in the pods," Daan says. "It doesn't read the colonists. It reads the chips." He pulls up the thermal overlay for Deck Fourteen. The image is a flat, uniform blue — no heat differential, no metabolic signatures, no variation between occupied and empty pods. "Nine kilowatts is enough to keep the monitoring chips powered. It is not enough to keep four hundred and twelve people frozen."

Yara looks at the thermal map. The pods her system has been reporting as occupied and stable are drawing enough power for their electronics and nothing else. The four hundred and twelve colonists her biometric sweep has been counting every shift are not in cryostasis. They have not been in cryostasis for nearly the entire transit.

> A = Yara (cryostasis technician); B = 412 colonists are in stable cryostasis on Deck Fourteen (confirmed by biometric sweep every shift, power steady since departure); E = Daan showing Deck Fourteen draws only 9 kW (not the 62 kW nominal), and the thermal overlay showing no metabolic signatures — the biometric sweep reads monitoring chips in pods, not colonists; B′ = the pods are not sustaining cryostasis — the colonists have not been in stasis for nearly the entire transit, and the biometric system has been reporting chip status, not human status. ✓ all four roles, ✓ all three states.

**Di-Sf4-3.** I have administered the seed vault on Kepler-186f for the full seven years of the colony's existence, and in seven years I have distributed seed stock to the agricultural teams on the understanding that our genebank contains twelve hundred unique cultivar lines — the full terrestrial biodiversity package loaded at Earth departure. That number is the foundation of the colony's long-term food-security model: enough genetic diversity to adapt crops to local soil chemistry over twenty generations of selective breeding. This morning I began the mandatory decadal inventory, pulling seed trays from cryogenic storage and scanning their genomic tags for the first time since landing. The first forty trays scanned as expected — forty unique lines, correctly labelled. Tray forty-one returned the same genomic signature as tray six. Tray forty-two matched tray eleven. I kept scanning. By tray two hundred I had found only sixty-three unique genomes. The remaining trays are duplicates — same cultivar lines, re-labelled with different variety codes, loaded into different slots. The twelve hundred lines I have been drawing down are sixty-three lines copied twenty times each. The genetic diversity the colony's food model depends on was never aboard this ship.

> A = I (seed vault administrator); B = the colony's genebank contains 1,200 unique cultivar lines (the full terrestrial biodiversity package, foundation of the twenty-generation food-security model); E = decadal genomic-tag inventory showing only 63 unique genomes — the remaining trays are duplicates re-labelled with different variety codes; B′ = the genebank holds 63 unique lines, not 1,200 — the genetic diversity required for long-term crop adaptation was never loaded. ✓ all four roles, ✓ all three states.

**Di-Sf4-4.** Station Director Reyes walks the perimeter of the antimatter containment ring on Orbital Fabrication Platform Helios-3, reading the magnetic-field indicators on each of the sixteen confinement cells. She has approved the platform's output certification every quarter for two years based on the automated containment report, which shows all sixteen cells active and holding stable antihydrogen quantities — the minimum threshold for the platform's operating license from the Transit Authority. Cell one: active, indicator green. Cell two: active. She works her way around the ring. Cells one through nine are green. Cell ten is dark. Not red, not amber — dark. Unpowered. She touches the housing. It is cold. She checks cell eleven. Dark and cold. Twelve through sixteen: all dark, all cold, all ambient temperature. Seven of the sixteen cells are unpowered and have been unpowered long enough to reach thermal equilibrium with the station hull. The automated containment report has been reading nine cells and reporting sixteen. The platform has been operating at fifty-six percent of its licensed containment capacity, and every output certification she has signed has attested to a confinement architecture that is barely more than half present.

> A = Station Director Reyes; B = all sixteen confinement cells in the antimatter containment ring are active and holding stable antihydrogen (per automated containment reports, basis of the platform's operating license and output certification); E = physical walk-around finding cells 10 through 16 dark, unpowered, and at ambient temperature — the automated report has been reading nine cells and reporting sixteen; B′ = only nine of sixteen cells are operational — the platform is at 56% of licensed containment capacity, and every certification she has signed overstates the confinement architecture. ✓ all four roles, ✓ all three states.

**Di-Sf4-5.** AUTOMATED DIAGNOSTIC TRANSCRIPT — DEEP-SPACE RELAY BEACON THETA-7
TECHNICIAN ON SITE: K. ODUYA, RELAY MAINTENANCE DIVISION
TIMESTAMP: 2847.114.09:22 UTC

ODUYA: Theta-7 has been listed as a primary node in the Outer Belt communication backbone for eleven years. All network routing tables treat it as a full-duplex relay with independent signal processing — meaning it receives transmissions from the inner system, processes them, and retransmits on a clean carrier to the outer stations. Every latency model and bandwidth allocation for the Outer Belt assumes Theta-7 is doing this work. I am here for the scheduled hardware refresh.

ODUYA: I have opened the main processing chassis. The signal-processing module is not installed. The mounting bracket is present, but the module slot is empty. There are no cable connections to the processing bus. I am checking the relay's transmission logs.

ODUYA: The transmission logs show Theta-7 receiving inner-system signals and retransmitting them without modification. It is operating as a passive repeater — amplifying and forwarding the raw signal without processing, error correction, or carrier separation. The network has been routing through it as though it were a full processing node, but it has been functioning as a signal mirror for its entire operational life. The bandwidth and error-correction capacity the Outer Belt routing tables attribute to this node has never existed. Every latency guarantee downstream of Theta-7 is based on processing that is not occurring.
